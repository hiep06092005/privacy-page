HealthAI Team ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Health AI mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and sensitive health data.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our App, in compliance with applicable data privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) and the Google Play Developer Program Policies (specifically the User Data Policy and Health Apps Policy).

By downloading, accessing, or using Health AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Important Medical Disclaimer

Health AI is designed exclusively for general wellness, fitness tracking, and lifestyle informational purposes. The App is NOT a medical device, nor does it provide medical diagnosis, medical advice, treatment, or clinical recommendations.

  • Any content, AI assistance, supplement logging, or fitness metrics provided by the App must not replace professional clinical judgment.
  • Always consult a physician or licensed healthcare provider before making medical decisions, starting new exercise routines, or taking dietary supplements.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your device, and via integrated features to deliver a personalized wellness experience:

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Full name, email address, password (hashed and encrypted), or authentication tokens if logging in via third-party providers (e.g., Google Sign-In).
  • User Profile & Demographic Metrics: Age, gender, height, weight, daily water intake goals, and fitness targets.
  • Health & Lifestyle Data: Dietary supplement intake schedules, supplement logs, hydration levels, activity logs, and personal health habits.

B. Device Sensors & Technical Permissions

Depending on the features you enable, our App may request the following permissions:

  • Camera & Photos (CAMERA, Photo Library): Used exclusively when you choose to take or upload photos (such as scanning supplement packaging, food logs, or setting a profile picture). We do not access your photo library without explicit user action.
  • Motion Sensors (Sensors / Accelerometer / Gyroscope): Used to detect movement, measure physical steps, and track daily activity.
  • Location Information (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): If granted, precise or approximate location is used exclusively during active outdoor workout tracking (e.g., walking, running distance). We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed unless explicitly requested and permitted for active workouts.
  • Local Storage (SharedPreferences): Used on your device to store offline preferences, theme settings, session tokens, and local cache.

C. Automatically Collected Usage & Log Data

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, language settings, and network connection status.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Data: Crash logs, performance metrics, and app error logs to ensure application stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate, transparent purposes:

  1. Delivering Core Services: Providing daily activity dashboards, tracking supplement schedules, calculating water intake requirements, and interactive mascot health feedback.
  2. Personalization: Customizing daily targets and habit reminders based on your registered physical metrics and health preferences.
  3. Social & Community Features (Optional): Allowing you to connect with friends, share activity progress, and participate in health leaderboards (only upon active opt-in).
  4. Security & Authentication: Verifying user accounts, preventing fraudulent logins, and ensuring the integrity of user sessions.
  5. App Improvement & Bug Fixing: Analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to troubleshoot issues and enhance app performance.

4. No Sale of Personal or Health Data

We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or commercialize your personal information, physical metrics, or sensitive health data to data brokers, advertising networks, or any third party.

We do not use sensitive health or fitness data for targeted behavioral advertising.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We will only disclose your data under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party cloud infrastructure, database hosting, and crash analytics providers. These vendors are bound by strict data processing agreements and are prohibited from using your data for any independent purpose.
  • With Your Explicit Consent: If you choose to share your progress or challenge achievements with friends within the App.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of our users or the public.

6. Data Security and Storage

We implement industry-standard physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the App and backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive credentials, tokens, and data stored in databases or local storage are encrypted.
  • Access Controls: Strict role-based access limits ensure that only authorized personnel have access to backend systems for operational maintenance.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

A. Retention Period

We retain your personal and health information only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the App's services.

B. User Rights & Complete Account/Data Deletion (Google Play Compliant)

You have the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Confirming this action will permanently erase your profile, health logs, supplement records, and credentials from our active databases.
  • Web / Email Request: You can also request complete account and personal data deletion by sending an email from your registered email address to thanhhoa120409@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process and permanently delete your records within 30 business days.

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete such data from our servers.
